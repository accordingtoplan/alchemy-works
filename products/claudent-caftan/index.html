Description
The Caftan is designed with a relaxed, straight silhouette and an easy drape through the body. Featuring a soft V neckline, short sleeves, and side slits for movement, it offers coverage with a light, open feel. Cut in Isola, Claudent’s vegan alternative to silk, the fabric is inspired by the light, ethereal breeze of an island escape. Isola is a vegan alternative that drapes and moves like silk, with inherent UPF 50+ protection woven into the fiber itself. Unlike silk, which relies on chemical treatments for sun protection, Isola offers lasting coverage by nature.
