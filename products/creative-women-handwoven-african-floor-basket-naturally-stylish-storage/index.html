Description
Designed for both movement and stability, this handwoven African pot basket features dual handles that make carrying, lifting, and everyday use feel effortless. Crafted in Ghana from durable, renewable elephant grass, each basket is woven by hand using traditional techniques passed down through generations. The double weave construction creates a strong, structured form that holds its shape beautifully over time. Its generous size and open shape make it ideal for storing blankets, laundry, toys, or daily essentials, while the breathable weave helps keep contents fresh.
