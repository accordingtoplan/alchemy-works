Description
Inspired by the automatism works of Ithell Colquhoun, this piece was formed by pouring liquid wax into water, in an effort to remove conscious projections and allowing an organic and perhaps subconscious shape to form. Cast in solid 925 silver and paired with a silver wire collar, the Lekane shape acts as a torque closure. This is a singular piece and cannot be duplicated or modified.
