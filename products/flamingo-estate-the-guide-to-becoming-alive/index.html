Last August I sat in the goat shed and started writing a book. It was the kind of book I NEEDED to read. I was hungry to learn about how to live a GREAT life — not just a good one. I wanted to hear directly from my heroes about how they found their way to happiness, greatness and joy. The Guide to Becoming Alive is part manifesto, part memoir.
