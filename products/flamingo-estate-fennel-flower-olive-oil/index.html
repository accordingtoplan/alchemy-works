Description
Fennel pollen is sometimes called “the spice of angels” — prized in Tuscany and Lazio for its golden hue and honeyed aroma. Our version begins with our own Heritage Extra Virgin Olive Oil — grown and pressed in Ojai, CA — infused in-house with hand-dried Fennel pollen from Carranza Family Farms, cultivated regeneratively along the California coast. A balanced play of flavor that captures the rich, peppery vibrancy of the Oil, mixed with the full floral complexity of the Fennel.
