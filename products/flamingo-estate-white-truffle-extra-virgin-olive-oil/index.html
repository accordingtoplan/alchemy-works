Description
16 fl oz / 750 mL This Summer, the idea for our White Truffle Olive Oil took root in the forests of Forcoli, Italy, where my mother and I joined the Savini family, who have been Truffle-hunting these woods for generations. Led by Luca Campinotti and his trio of extraordinary dogs, we spent the morning slipping through dappled light and fallen leaves, watching the dogs circle and scratch, then gently unearth the treasure below. The scent of warm earth and oak leaves, the thrill of discovery — became the genesis of this Olive Oil.White Truffles are nature’s greatest riddle.
