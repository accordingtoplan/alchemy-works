Description
A limited production, functional object d'art exclusively produced by Anastasio Home. The 810 is a sculptural eight by ten inch tray cut from a single piece of solid marble or stone with scalloped edges, hand-finished by artisans in a growing stone atelier in Rajasthan, India. Dimensions: 10"L x 8" W x .5"H Inner dish: 7" L x 5" W Subtle scallop variations and patterns should be expected and embraced as this is a hand-finished object made of natural stone
