Description
ROAM’s ‘Raffy Cuffs’ slip on sandal is a perfect mix of earthy elegance and contemporary design, with a handwoven raffia upper featuring cutout straps designed to artfully frame the foot. The neoprene upper lining softly wicks away moisture. Our custom contoured footbed comfortably molds to your feet and is lined in moisture wicking neoprene. The durable, high-flex, tread outsole provides stability and is made of sustainable BIO EVA derived from sugarcane, something we can all feel good about as we ROAM. ROAMs fit true to size for a flop-free walk.
