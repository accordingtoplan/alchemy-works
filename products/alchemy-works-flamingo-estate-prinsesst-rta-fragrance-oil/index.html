Description
Inspired by the famous Swedish Princess cake from Sant Ambroeus on the Upper East Side Bakery, the Prinsesstårta Fragrance Oil is pure deliciousness. Once applied, the scent lasts and evolves throughout the day, becoming a fragrant love letter to the famous cake that, as legend has it, won the hearts of the Swedish princesses in the 1920s. Now, it can win yours too. It blends the intoxicating oils of creamy Heliotrope Flowers, earthy Oakmoss, and musky Amber in a base of sweet Almond Oil — releasing a fragrance reminiscent of Marzipan, Vanilla custard, exotic spices.
