Description
Creamy porcelain candle. Scent is a very light, lovely orange blossom that is reminiscent of 1960s Los Angeles backyards on June evenings. A collaboration between Isabel Halley Ceramics and Prosperity Candle. Based in western Massachusetts, Prosperity Candle employs women with refugee status in the United States and invests in women to create change and sustainable impact. Once your candle is done burning it is meant to be used for your most special things. Isabel Halley recommends putting your toothbrush, razor, makeup brushes, and other top shelf goodies
