Description
Bricia Lopez’s Oaxaca is a colorful celebration of Oaxacan cuisine from her family’s landmark Guelaguetza restaurant in Los Angeles, co-written with food writer Javier Cabral. Oaxaca is the culinary heart of Mexico, the land of the seven moles, the birthplace of mezcal, and the home of so many rich flavors—from the corn to the chiles, herbs, spices, and chocolate. Guelaguetza is a James Beard Award–winning restaurant that has been the center of life for the Oaxacan community in Los Angeles for more than 25 years, founded by Oaxacan immigrants and run by the same family today.
