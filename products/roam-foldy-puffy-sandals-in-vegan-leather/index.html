Description
ROAM’s ‘Foldy Puffy’ is made in buttery vegan leather and lined in breathable, moisture wicking neoprene. Memory foam is inserted by hand into the upper which creates extreme comfort and allows the shoe to mold to your foot for a perfectly cushioned and supportive fit. Featuring a contoured comfort footbed, covered in neoprene to keep feet comfortable and dry. The durable tread outsole provides stability and is made of sustainable BIO EVA derived from sugarcane, something we can all feel good about as we ROAM.
