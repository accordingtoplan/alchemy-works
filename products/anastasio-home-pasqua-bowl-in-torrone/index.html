Description
A love note to the beautiful braided breads of Easter made by the founder’s nonna every Spring. Pane di Pasqua as it’s known. A limited production, functional object d'art exclusively produced by Anastasio Home. The Pasqua Bowl is a sculptural 8” inch catchall cut from a single piece of solid marble or stone with wide, braided border, hand-finished by artisans in a growing stone atelier in Rajasthan, India. Dimensions: 8"L x 8" W x 2” H Subtle finish variations and patterns should be expected and embraced as this is a hand-finished object made of natural stone
