Raina Lee, in the treehouse

A writer and ceramicist in Mount Washington. Her pots live in a treehouse on the hill behind the house.

Sample story

Raina Lee lives on a wooded hillside in Mount Washington, Los Angeles. The house came with a small treehouse when she bought it in 2013.

A wooden path and a run of steps climb the hill to reach it.

The treehouse is her gallery. Shelves cover the walls. Pots sit in rows by colour: chalk white, moss green, cobalt, a black that looks wet.

The making happens closer to the house. The garage holds the wheel and the glazes. Raku firings happen on the narrow patio out back.

Lee is a writer too. Years ago she self-published a zine about video game culture. The same curiosity runs through the glazes. Old techniques, pushed until the surfaces turn strange.

Photographs by Justin Chung, first published in Faculty Department
