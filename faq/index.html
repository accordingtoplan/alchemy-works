Questions

Returns, pick up, shipping, store hours and gift cards.

How long is the return window?
Ten days from delivery to ask for a return. Pieces bought in December 2026 get 30 days. They need to come back unworn and unused, with tags, in the original packaging and with the receipt. Start a return at store@alchemyworks.us. Refunds go back to the original payment within 10 business days of approval.
What can't be returned?
Vintage, swim, sale pieces, gift cards, custom or personalised jewelry, and Marian Paquette and Teresa Foglia. Fine jewelry is exchange only. The full returns policy has the details.
Can an online order be picked up in store?
Yes, at all four stores. Order online and collect in two hours from a store that has the piece. Every product page shows which stores have it today.
How much is shipping?
Free within the US on orders over $100. Otherwise $10 for standard shipping. Orders leave within three business days and arrive five to eight business days later. Shipping policy.
When are the stores open?
Signal, Arts District, Los Angeles: daily, 11am to 7pm
Harbor House, Lido Marina Village, Newport Beach: Sunday to Wednesday 10am to 6pm; Thursday to Saturday 10am to 7pm
Yountville, Napa Valley: Monday to Thursday 11am to 5pm; Friday and Saturday 10am to 6pm; Sunday 10am to 5pm
Sea Shop, Carmel by the Sea: daily, 10am to 5pm.
All four stores.
Are there gift cards?
Yes. Digital or printed, good online and in all four stores. Gift cards can't be returned.
Where do questions go?
store@alchemyworks.us, 10am to 5pm Pacific time. Each store also has its own phone and email, on its store page.
