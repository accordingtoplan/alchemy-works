About

Since 2013

Alchemy Works opened in November 2013 on Third Street in the Arts District, a few doors from Apolis. A 1959 Fiat Abarth sat on the shop floor, always for sale. The idea was to stock what Raan and Lindsay Parton couldn't find in Los Angeles, and give the lesser known makers a wall.

Thirteen years later there are four stores, from the Arts District to Lido Marina Village, Yountville and a pink cottage on Ocean Avenue in Carmel. Retail, gallery and events, with a new show on the wall every eight weeks.
