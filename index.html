Since 2013Arts District · Newport Beach · Yountville · Carmel

Alchemy Works opened in November 2013 on Third Street in the Arts District, a few doors from Apolis. A 1959 Fiat Abarth sat on the shop floor, always for sale. The idea was to stock what Raan and Lindsay Parton couldn't find in Los Angeles, and give the lesser known makers a wall. Thirteen years later there are four shops, from Lido Marina Village to a pink cottage on Ocean Avenue.

Alchemy Works x Apolis

Apolis means global citizen. The jute market bags are made in Bangladesh by women artisans and printed with the name of a place. The brand started a few doors from the first Alchemy Works. A proposed edition carries the names of the four shops.

Rooms that come together slowly.

Half of every shop is home. Tina Frey resin, Isabel Halley ceramics, Sandoval and Earl Home, Flamingo Estate on the counter, and vintage found on the road. Chosen the way a house fills up over years, one piece at a time.
